NYRQELO for Bluesky

Keep the posts you want to revisit.

Save selected Bluesky posts in your browser, search your collection, and add your own notes. Available on the Chrome Web Store.

Features

  • Save post text, author details, and links to the original Bluesky posts.
  • Search post text, accounts, hashtags, and private notes.
  • Filter saved posts and sort by save date.
  • Add private notes, export and restore JSON backups, and save reports as PDF through the browser print dialog.
  • Switch between light and dark themes and use the interface in English or another supported language.

Media files are not permanently archived: images, video links, and thumbnails depend on their original hosting services. This is an independent extension, not an official Bluesky product.

Getting started

  1. Install NYRQELO and open Bluesky.
  2. Click “Save to NYRQELO” on a post.
  3. Open NYRQELO from the browser toolbar.
  4. Search, filter, and add notes to organize your saved posts.

Information used and its purpose

The extension processes the text, display names, handles, post URLs, avatar and image reference URLs, video-related links, thumbnail reference URLs, save dates, and private notes for posts you choose to save. It also processes language, theme, search, and sorting preferences. This information is used to save, search, filter, annotate, back up, and display your saved posts.

Storage and exports

Information is stored in your browser's local extension storage. JSON backup exports write saved posts and notes to a file that you save. PDF reports use your browser's print function.

External connections

Displaying images, avatars, and thumbnails connects to the services hosting those files. Those services receive information normally associated with web requests, such as your IP address. Developer-support links open Ko-fi. Original-post and video links open their respective destinations. External services handle information under their own policies.

Use of information

Saved posts and notes are not sold or used for purposes unrelated to this extension's post-saving and organization functions. They are not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. The reviewed implementation does not send saved posts or notes to a developer-operated server, serve advertisements, or include an analytics service.

Deletion and control

You can delete individual saved posts or clear the saved history. Notes can be edited. Removing the extension or deleting your browser profile may remove locally stored data. You control and delete exported backup files yourself.
